Privacy Policy

Academic Assistant is a college academic information assistant that provides answers based on official academic documents.

Information We Collect

When you use Academic Assistant through WhatsApp, we may receive your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send to the service.

How We Use Information

Your messages are used only to process your academic questions and provide relevant answers from the documents available to the assistant.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes. Messages may be processed by the services required to operate the assistant, such as WhatsApp/Meta and the AI service used to generate responses.

Data Retention

We do not intentionally retain WhatsApp conversations longer than necessary to operate and maintain the service.

Third-Party Services

Academic Assistant uses WhatsApp/Meta services to receive and send WhatsApp messages and may use an AI service to process academic questions.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the Academic Assistant service, please contact the project administrator.
